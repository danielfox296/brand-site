What Music Actually Does

Every track carries decisions. Most stores make none of them.

Tempo

Slower tempos extend dwell time. Faster tempos clear the floor. Unmanaged, it defaults to whatever the artist felt.

Key & Mode

Major keys lift mood. Minor keys create introspection. The wrong one changes what customers decide.

Instrumentation

Acoustic signals craft. Synths signal trend. Each instrument sets a brand expectation before a product is touched.

Lyrics

A lyric about leaving primes departure. A lyric about indulgence primes purchase. Words are never neutral.

Familiarity

Familiar songs make time feel faster. The customer thinks she has been there longer. She wants to leave sooner.

The Customer
Mara.

She arrives on Saturday afternoons. She is in no hurry.

She picks things up, puts them down, picks them up again. She is not deciding whether she can afford it. She is deciding whether it belongs in her life.

She finds busy soundscapes irritating. She needs the room to feel like it has time.

Typical visit 18–24 minutes
Frequency 2× per month, Saturday afternoons
Decision style Deliberate. Needs space to feel certain.
Audio response Responds to sound that does not compete
Peak engagement When the environment feels unhurried
She stays 22% longer when the room feels unhurried.
Musical prescription
TEMPO  68–82 BPM MODE  Major, soft voicings RHYTHM  Acoustic, no drive LYRICAL  Ambient or abstract

The Story

Mara's 18 minutes.

The music shifted four times. Each shift was a decision.

Track 1 of 4
0:00
Entry

Mara walks in. The music is already there. It does not announce itself.

80 BPM, acoustic texture, major key, muted voicings. No percussion. The room says welcome before she reads the price tag.

▶  Start the Wonder player from the beginning.
4:32
Active Browse

She has picked up three things. The system detected movement and engagement. The music shifted.

94 BPM, light rhythmic pulse. Same temperature as Entry. Slightly more present. Keeps pace without leading.

▶  Skip to 4:32 in the Wonder player.
11:15
Extended Dwell

She has not moved from the ceramics shelf in 90 seconds. The system detected it. The music slowed.

68 BPM. Major sevenths and suspended chords. No percussion. The moment belongs to her, not to the store.

▶  Skip to 11:15 in the Wonder player.
16:48
Exit Approach

She started toward the door. She had not bought anything yet. The music shifted again.

87 BPM. More textural. A melodic idea that does not resolve. A question, not a conclusion.
