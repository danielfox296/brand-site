Daniel Fox
Founder

Daniel bootstrapped Skreened to eight-figure annual revenue in six years. 180 employees, multiple production facilities, and dual recognition in 2013 as one of Ohio's fastest-growing and best places to work. After exiting, he spent years on the technical side of music, the question of why particular sounds produce particular responses in people. Entuned is where retail and music meet. Denver, CO.

Mrinmayi Katti
Data Scientist

Mrinmayi builds the data infrastructure. At Loxo, she designed distributed migration workflows processing 200K+ records per run at 99%+ integrity, connected seven external data sources, and shipped containerized pipelines on Kubernetes. Her work at Reliance Jio put her inside A/B testing at scale, where her pipelines contributed to an 18% retention lift. At Entuned, she owns the pipeline that connects in-store behavior to transaction data. B.S. Data Science, NJIT, 3.8 GPA. Dean's List. Merit Scholar.
