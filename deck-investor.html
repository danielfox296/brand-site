Investor Presentation

Retail audio, taken seriously.

entuned.co

The Problem

Every retail store plays music. Almost nobody measures it.

Chains pay for generic streaming. The music gets chosen for reasons that have nothing to do with what happens in the store.

No visibility into whether the music helps or hurts sales.

The real cost is the revenue lost every day to music that has nothing to do with the customer in front of it.

The Insight

Music is a proven behavioral lever. The commercial category has ignored four decades of research.

Milliman 1982 on tempo and pace. North and colleagues 1999 on genre and choice. Areni and Kim 1993 on music and spending. Andersson and colleagues 2012 on volume and restaurant revenue.

Why Now

Two things changed in 2024 that made this company possible.

Generative AI Music

Generative AI music hit production quality in 2024. For the first time, retail audio can be produced to match a specific store and a specific customer without catalog constraints or licensing exposure.

Retail Analytics APIs

Retail analytics platforms now expose store-level behavioral data over API. Foot traffic. Dwell time. Zone transitions. The sensor layer already exists in thousands of stores. Nobody was acting on it through the audio surface. That is the gap.

The Real Gap

The category competes on catalogs and dayparting. Playing different music at lunch than at 9am is the most sophisticated thing the incumbents do. None of them measure whether any of it actually works.

No existing service ties its operation to the retail analytics stack. None are positioned on conversion or dwell time. That ceiling is where Entuned's floor begins.

White Space

The streaming giants and the retail analytics platforms have both ignored this intersection.

The streaming giants have the music. The retail analytics platforms have the data. Nobody has connected them. That gap is the company.

The Ask

Pre-seed round open to reach proof of lift.

Three to five pilot partners. Twelve months. Treatment and control store pairs. At the end, we either have statistically significant evidence that retail audio moves the numbers, or we have a clean answer that the thesis does not hold in the conditions we tested.

Either outcome is worth the raise. Optionality to expand, license, or raise Series A on real data.
