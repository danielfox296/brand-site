For Cosmetics Retail

The audio at the tester wall is shaping the decision.

Beauty retail lives on testing behavior, dwell, and the customer's sense of whether the brand is worth the premium. All three respond to the audio environment. The dedicated cosmetics framing here is being rewritten — talk to us in the meantime if you operate beauty retail at multi-store scale.

What Still Applies

The product works for cosmetics. The framing on this page does not.

What we are reworking on this page is the ICP framing. The May 2026 prospect tier list places different cosmetics operators at different levels of fit, and the prior dedicated cosmetics framing did not capture that.
