The Soundtrack Is Shaping the Decision.

A customer stops at the tester wall, picks up a lipstick, turns it over, looks up. They decide in the next thirty seconds whether to open it or walk away. Something on the speakers is shaping that decision, and nobody on your team is tracking what.

What We Measure

Beauty retail lives and dies on testing behavior, dwell, and the customer's sense of whether this brand is worth the premium. All three respond to audio, and the public research is direct about it. Gueguen and Jacob (2013) measured a 28.6 percent increase in customer spending in a retail setting when the music fit the mood of the store, from EUR 25.31 to EUR 32.55 per transaction. That was a flower shop. Beauty, where every decision is a sensory one, is at least as responsive. Entuned measures what actually happens on your floor when the music matches the customer.

Dwell Time by Department

How long customers spend at the tester wall, the fragrance counter, and the treatment stations. The customer who stays longer tests more, and the customer who tests more buys more. Music shapes the sense of time, and a shopper who feels unhurried stays for the second product.

Product Interaction Rate

Whether customers move from looking to testing. Yalch and Spangenberg (2000) found that familiar music made shoppers leave 8 percent faster. The recognizable hit tells the customer time is passing. That is exactly the opposite of what a beauty counter needs from its audio.

Average Transaction Value

Whether customers add a second or third item. Areni and Kim (1993) showed that classical music shifted customers toward higher-priced wine by raising the perceived quality of the room, not the product. The same mechanism runs in beauty. The customer who feels they are in a premium environment reaches for the premium item.

Perceived Quality Alignment

Beauty is an identity purchase, and the customer reads the whole room. Andersson et al. (2012) measured 601 real transactions and found that music that clashed with the store actively reduced willingness to pay. A disconnect between what the store looks like and what the store sounds like undoes the work your merchandising team spent months on.

How Entuned Works for Cosmetics Retail

We start with your customer, not with "beauty shoppers" as a demographic. Who the store is for, what that person responds to, how they want a beauty purchase to feel. That becomes the foundation for every musical choice we make.

Every track is original and license-free. The music is built to encourage testing, reinforce the quality positioning your brand has earned, and keep a customer on the floor long enough to discover the second thing.

We work with retail analytics platforms where you already have them. When we change the audio and dwell time shifts in the same store the same week, you have a measurement connected to a decision, not a vendor story.

Implementation Overview

Week One

We learn your customer, your brand, and your competitive context. You share any existing analytics access and a floor plan if you have one.

Weeks Two and Three

First soundtrack built and shared for your team to review. You give notes on fit and energy. We refine before it goes live.

Week Four

Go live. Streaming connects to your existing speakers via Bluetooth or AirPlay. Measurement through your POS and analytics begins the day the music starts.

Ongoing

Fresh music on a monthly cadence, informed by what the numbers did the month before. Dwell, conversion, and ticket tracked against the audio that produced them.

What we need from you

POS access for measurement. A floor plan if you have one. RetailNext or equivalent analytics access if available. Any brand guidelines or audio direction you want us to work inside.
