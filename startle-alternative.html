Credit where it's due

What Startle gets right

Startle is the incumbent that takes music most seriously as a behavioral variable — and that deserves credit. Three things they do well:

They respect the science

Startle's musicologists talk tempo, arousal, and valence, and their content engages the same behavioral literature Entuned engineers against. The closest any incumbent comes to treating music as a variable instead of decoration.

Real multi-site control

Their Studio platform gives head office genuine central control with role-based permissions that stop staff overrides — a real answer to the "every store sounds different" problem.

Managed service depth

Hardware, professional installation, proactive monitoring, and 24/7/365 support. For operators who want a vendor to own the whole audio stack, the service layer is real.

For multi-location operators

Where Startle falls short

Four structural problems, starting with the one their own homepage sets up.

Outcome claims, no outcome instrument

Playlists "designed to drive specific outcomes, like energy, dwell time, and sales" — but no pilot, no control stores, no per-door read. The proof on offer is testimonials about vibe and service quality, not numbers.

The curation ceiling

Startle selects from licensed catalog — songs whose tempo, lyrics, and cultural reference were fixed by the original artist for other reasons. Curation can only choose among those tracks. It can't specify them.

Hardware and install cycle

The Startle Player ships with professional installation — a rollout project at every site. Well executed, but it's a procurement-shaped process for something a browser can do in minutes.

Quote-based pricing

No published rates — a discovery call and a quote before you know what a location costs. Fine for enterprise buying; friction for an operator who wants to hear it on the floor this week.

What Entuned does instead

Free forever, published pricing, zero hardware. Original music composed to your outcome — the parameters specified, not selected for. And the outcome gets measured: a free 12-week pilot across matched test and control stores, read in your own reporting.

The claims audit

Startle talks outcomes. The loop never closes.

Startle is the only incumbent whose homepage names the same outcomes Entuned engineers for — energy, dwell time, sales. Take that as validation: the category is moving from licensing compliance to behavior, and the most scientifically serious incumbent knows it.

Now audit the claim. What sits underneath it is a curation model — musicologists scoring licensed tracks on energy and mood — and what sits after it is nothing. No measurement in your stores, no control comparison, no per-door number. Designing toward an outcome you never measure isn't a design spec; it's a mood board with a thesis.

One question separates the two products: what did the music contribute in your stores last quarter? Entuned's answer is a free 12-week pilot across matched test and control stores, read in your own reporting. If a vendor designs for outcomes, the outcome should have a number.

Common questions

Switching from Startle

Can I keep my Startle service while testing Entuned?
Yes. Entuned Free is $0 with no credit card, so you can run it in one store as a parallel test while Startle stays live everywhere else. Running 5–50 locations? The stronger comparison is a free 12-week measured pilot across matched test and control stores — start a measured pilot.
Isn't Startle's behavioral science the same thing Entuned does?
The science is the same literature — and Startle deserves credit for engaging it seriously. The difference is what happens on either side of it. Before: Startle curates from a licensed catalog, so the tempo, lyrics, and cultural reference of every track were fixed by someone else; Entuned composes the track to specification. After: Startle never measures the outcome in your stores; Entuned's pilot reads it in your own reporting, store by store against control.
Will I owe ASCAP, BMI, or SESAC if I switch to Entuned?
No, not for music played through Entuned. We compose and own every track in our catalog outright, so there is no PRO obligation passed to the operator for audio Entuned delivers. Other audio sources you run keep their own licensing posture.
Startle installs and manages the hardware. What replaces that with Entuned?
Nothing needs to. The Entuned player runs in any modern browser at music.entuned.co on hardware you already have — a tablet, a mini-PC, anything connected to your amplifier. No install appointment, no proprietary player, nothing to service when it fails.
How fast can I roll Entuned out across all my locations?
Each location takes minutes — the player is a URL. Multi-location operators self-serve through app.entuned.co/start and manage all sites from one dashboard login.
Is there a usage limit on Entuned Free?
Entuned Free is a real free tier — no credit card, no trial timer, intended for ongoing use by single-location operators. The differences with paid tiers live on the pricing page; nothing on the free tier expires.
