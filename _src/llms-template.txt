# Entuned

> Entuned is a retail music strategy platform that turns customer psychology into store soundtracks — driving measurable sales lift. Not licensed catalog. Not a playlist service. Self-serve, starts free.

Entuned makes original music for retail stores. An owner-operator signs up free at app.entuned.co/start, picks an outcome (more dwell, more energy, bigger baskets), and the music plays in-browser the same day — no hardware, no contract, no card. Because the music is original and carries no PRO obligation, it removes the ASCAP/BMI/SESAC/GMR licensing question entirely. Paid tiers (Boost, $99/location/month; Professional, $399/location/month) tailor the music to the store's ideal customer profile and tie it to sales outcomes.

The category we operate in sits between the traditional in-store music business (Mood Media, Soundtrack Your Brand, SiriusXM Business, Cloud Cover) and the retail analytics business (RetailNext, Sensormatic, Standard AI). Those two categories have never shared data. That gap is where audio fails to show up as a measured variable in retail performance.

## Research Foundation

Four decades of peer-reviewed research on music and consumer behavior sit behind the category. Milliman (1982) established that tempo changes shopper pace and spending. Areni and Kim (1993) showed classical music in a wine shop moved customers toward more expensive bottles. Yalch and Spangenberg (2000) documented that familiar music shortens visits. These findings have been replicated across retail formats and decades.
