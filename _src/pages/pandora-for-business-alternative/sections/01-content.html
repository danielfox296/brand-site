What Pandora for Business charges

Two products, two pricing structures

The version sold and supported by Mood Media is $29.95/mo monthly or $23.96/mo annual per location, zone, and device, with a $99 player (free on annual plans, $49 on monthly). (Pandora via Mood Media)

Pandora CloudCover — the version sold direct at pandora.com/business after the March 2024 rebrand — lists at roughly $16.95/mo on prepaid annual, plus a $99.50 CloudBox per zone. CloudCover added a digital signage product in 2025. SiriusXM Music for Business, the sister product, lists at $26.95/mo with a 30-day free trial. Each location, zone, or device requires its own subscription.

BYOD via the Pandora app is supported on both versions, though the marketing pushes hardware.

Credit where it's due

What Pandora for Business gets right

Pandora's recommendation engine is one of the best in the world at what it does — building a long-running station from a seed and keeping it interesting for hours of listening. For a single owner-operator who just wants "music that doesn't repeat all day, give me a brand to seed it from, and stop," that's a real benefit. The CloudCover hardware is dependable, the licensing posture is clean, and the product is genuinely simple to set up in one location.

For multi-location operators

Where Pandora for Business falls short

  • Algorithmic drift across locations. Pandora's stations are seeded and then evolve based on local listening behavior. Two locations with the same starting station won't sound the same after a week. For a multi-store brand trying to sound like one brand, that's a structural problem, not a tuning problem.
  • Per-location, per-zone, per-device billing. Both the Mood-resold and direct CloudCover versions charge per location/zone/device. A store with three zones is three subscriptions plus three boxes (or three BYOD setups).
  • Hardware push. Both products support BYOD via the Pandora app, but the sales motion is built around the player ($99 with Mood, $99.50 CloudBox direct). It's another box to lose, troubleshoot, and replace at every location.
  • No published in-store sales-lift methodology. Pandora for Business markets atmosphere and recognizability, but we could not find a published methodology for measuring the basket-size or dwell-time lift their stations produce inside an operator's store.
  • Two products under one brand name. The "Pandora for Business" sold by Mood Media is a different SKU and has different terms than the CloudCover product sold direct at pandora.com/business. Operators routinely conflate the two and end up with the wrong contract.

Common questions

Switching from Pandora for Business

Can I keep my Pandora for Business account while testing Entuned?
Yes. Entuned Free is $0 with no card, so you can run it on a tablet or laptop in one zone of one store while your Pandora subscription stays active. We recommend doing exactly that for a couple of weeks — comparing two locations side by side makes the drift problem obvious in a way pricing pages can't.
Do I have to keep the CloudBox or Mood player to use Entuned?
No. Entuned has no proprietary hardware — the player runs in a normal browser. You can repurpose any browser-capable device (an old iPad, a Chromecast-equipped TV, a small mini-PC behind the bar) and the CloudBox or Mood Harmony player can be returned or unplugged once Pandora is cancelled.
Will I owe ASCAP, BMI, or SESAC if I switch to Entuned?
No, not for music played through Entuned. We compose and own every track in our catalog outright, so there is no PRO obligation passed to the operator for audio Entuned delivers. Live performances or other audio sources keep their own licensing posture.
How fast can I roll Entuned out across all my locations?
Each location takes minutes — the player is a URL on a browser-capable device. Multi-location operators self-serve through app.entuned.co/start and use the customer dashboard to manage all sites from one login. There's no shipping, no install appointment, and the same programming plays in every store.
Is there a usage limit on Entuned Free?
Entuned Free is a real free tier — no card, no trial timer, intended for ongoing use by single-location operators who don't need the deeper customization or measurement of the paid tiers. The differences are documented on the pricing page; nothing on the free tier expires.
Can I seed Entuned from a Pandora station I like?
Sort of, but better. Instead of seeding from a station, Entuned interviews your space, your customer, and your daypart structure, and engineers programming around the parameters that drive retail outcomes — pace, energy arc, lyrical density, harmonic feel. You can absolutely point us at a Pandora station as a reference; we'll translate what's working about it into something owned and consistent.
