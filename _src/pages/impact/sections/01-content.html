Methodology

These figures represent the music-attributable share of revenue outcomes, not total potential lift from all variables. Based on peer-reviewed literature on acoustic environment and consumer behavior (Milliman 1982; Yalch & Spangenberg 2000; Knöferle et al. 2012; Renwick & Woolhouse 2023).

Transaction value component: Tempo, mode, and volume affect arousal and purchase depth. Conservative range: 2–5% ATV improvement on optimized transactions.

UPT component: Extended dwell time increases inventory exposure. Conservative range: 1–3% UPT improvement.

These ranges are intentionally below published study averages. A pilot replaces them with measured outcomes from your locations.
