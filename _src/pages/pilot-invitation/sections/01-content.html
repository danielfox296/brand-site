How It Works

Four steps. Ninety days. Real numbers.

Step 01

We learn your brand.

Your store, your customers, your aesthetic. We study what makes your space yours and use that as the foundation for everything we compose.

Step 02

We design your soundtrack.

Every track is built using research-backed methods. Tempo, energy, mood, and lyrical content calibrated to influence how long people stay and how much they spend.

Step 03

You play it.

Stream from any device you already have. AirPlay, Bluetooth, aux cable. Press play in the morning. The music handles itself from there.

Step 04

We measure what happens.

You share weekly sales data however works best for you. We run it against your historical comps and show you exactly what changed.

The Research

This isn't a guess.

Forty years of peer-reviewed research shows that when the audio environment fits the room, shoppers stay longer, browse more, and spend more per visit. Tempo, mode, harmonic texture, and even lyrical content all affect purchase behavior in ways that have been measured and replicated across dozens of studies.

The problem has always been measurement. Nobody has connected what's playing to what's selling at the transaction level. That's what we're building. Every track in your pilot catalogue is designed using these methods, specified at the variable level, so we can identify exactly what moved your numbers and why.

We're not competing with your taste. We're incorporating it into a soundtrack designed to lift your sales.

What We Need

Almost nothing.

A device to stream from. An iPad, a phone, a laptop. Anything that connects to your speakers via AirPlay, Bluetooth, or aux.

Weekly sales data. However is easiest. A POS export, a screenshot, a forwarded email. We'll work with whatever format you have.

Historical comps. Year-over-year, month-over-month, or week-over-week. We use your past numbers as the baseline so we can measure what changes.

One conversation. Fifteen to twenty minutes so we can understand your brand, your customer, and what a good outcome looks like for you.

The Pilot

90 days. No cost. No contract.

At the end of the pilot you get a report showing exactly what happened. If the numbers are worth continuing, we'll talk about it. If they're not, you keep the data and the music. No obligation either way.

Your Data

What we see. What we don't.

We run the music and your sales numbers side by side, anonymized, to look for correlation between what was playing and what sold. We need totals: revenue by day, transaction count, average ticket. That's it.

We don't need customer names, individual transaction details, or any personally identifying information. Our ingestion is separate from anything that could identify your customers. We're measuring the music, not the people.
