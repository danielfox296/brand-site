How to Increase Dwell Time in Retail Stores

Dwell time is the measurement of how long customers spend in your store. The longer they browse, the more products they see, and the more likely they are to buy. A retail-analytics industry estimate puts the relationship at roughly a 1.3% increase in sales for every 1% increase in dwell time.

Key Takeaways

• Industry estimate: a 1% increase in dwell time correlates with ~1.3% increase in sales
• Music tempo directly controls walking speed — Milliman study, replicated across retail formats
• Slow tempo + minor key + sparse arrangement produces the strongest dwell extension
• Music compresses perceived time — 20 minutes of browsing feels like 10 in the right environment
