What this checks

Errors must be fixed before recording. Warnings are advisory — they surface patterns that often weaken short-form delivery, but you can decide.

Click for the full rule set
  • Structure: requires Hook, Behavioral Effect, and Verdict sections at minimum.
  • Word budgets: Hook ≤ 12, Context ≤ 12, Behavioral Effect ≤ 30, Retail Implication ≤ 18, Verdict ≤ 10, CTA ≤ 14. Total target ≤ 90 words.
  • Hedge words: "I think", "kinda", "sort of", "maybe", "might", "to me", "actually", "honestly", "probably" — must be cut.
  • Audiophile vocabulary: "mix", "groove", "riff", "timbre", "production", "mastering" — flagged as not landing with retail operators.
  • Music → behavior translation: words like "fast", "slow", "catchy", "familiar" should pair with the behavioral consequence ("accelerates pace", "extends dwell", "attention theft", etc).
  • Named concepts: at least one from the Entuned vocabulary should appear per episode (Attention Theft, Behavioral Tempo, Identity Congruence, Familiarity Saturation, Dwell Engineering, etc).
  • Hook mechanism: the Hook should hit at least one of: money, contradiction, instant fact, authority, provocation, direct behavioral claim.
