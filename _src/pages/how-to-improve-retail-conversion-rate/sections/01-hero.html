How to Improve Your Retail Store Conversion Rate

Conversion rate in physical retail is the percentage of store visitors who make a purchase. If 100 people walk in and 20 buy something, your conversion rate is 20%. Industry benchmarks for specialty retail typically fall between 15-30%, depending on location, format, and foot traffic mix.

The gap between browsers and buyers is where most of your unrealized revenue sits. Getting more people through the door costs marketing dollars. Getting more of the people already inside your store to buy costs almost nothing, if you know which levers to pull.

The standard levers are staff training, store layout, checkout friction, and merchandising. There's a fifth lever that most retailers never consider: the ambient environment the customer is making their purchase decision inside of.
