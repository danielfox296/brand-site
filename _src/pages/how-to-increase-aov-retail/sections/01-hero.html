How to Increase Average Order Value in Retail Stores

Most of the advice online about increasing average order value is written for ecommerce. Upsell widgets, cart thresholds, bundled shipping incentives. Useful if you run a Shopify store. Less useful if you're standing behind a register watching customers buy one thing and leave.

Physical retail has its own AOV mechanics, and they're rooted in the in-store experience: how long someone browses, what catches their eye on the way to checkout, whether the environment makes them feel like spending or like rushing through.

The standard playbook, merchandising, cross-selling, loyalty programs, is well-documented. What isn't well-documented is the role of sound. The music playing in your store is either priming customers to spend more or it's doing nothing at all, and most store owners have no idea which.
