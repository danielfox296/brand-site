What Music Actually Does

Every track carries decisions. Most stores make none of them.

Tempo

Slower tempos extend dwell time. Faster tempos clear the floor. Unmanaged, it defaults to whatever the artist felt.

Key & Mode

Major keys lift mood. Minor keys create introspection. The wrong one changes what customers decide.

Instrumentation

Acoustic signals craft. Synths signal trend. Each instrument sets a brand expectation before a product is touched.

Lyrics

A lyric about leaving primes departure. A lyric about indulgence primes purchase. Words are never neutral.

Familiarity

Familiar songs make time feel faster. The customer thinks she has been there longer. She wants to leave sooner.

Entuned makes all of it intentional.
