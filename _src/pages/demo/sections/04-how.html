How This Works

From customer profile to in-store sound.

1

You describe your ideal customer. Who they are, how they shop, what state of mind supports their purchase decision.

2

Entuned translates that profile into musical parameters: tempo, harmonic density, rhythmic texture, instrumentation, production style. These parameters are designed to induce the psychological state your customer needs.

3

The system generates original music to those specifications. No licensing. No playlist curation. Music built for your store.

4

The music plays in your store. Your existing traffic measurement system records behavioral outcomes: dwell time, conversion, basket size. Entuned correlates the musical parameters with the results.

5

Over time, the correlation engine learns what works. The musical specification tightens. The lift compounds.
