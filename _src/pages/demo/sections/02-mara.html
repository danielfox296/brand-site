The Customer
Meet Mara.

She arrives on Saturday afternoons, usually after brunch. She’s in no hurry. She touches everything she’s considering. She’s not deciding whether she can afford it — she’s deciding whether it belongs in her life. She responds to music that doesn’t demand her attention. She finds busy soundscapes irritating.

Typical visit 18–24 minutes
Frequency 2× per month, Saturday afternoons
Decision style Deliberate — needs space to feel certain
Audio response Responds to sound that doesn’t compete for attention
Peak engagement When the environment feels unhurried

“She stays 22% longer when the room feels unhurried.”

What the system learned

Mara needs acoustic simplicity during decision windows. The system reduces rhythmic density when dwell time exceeds 60 seconds. It never introduces percussion during extended product engagement. The music becomes the room — not something in it.
