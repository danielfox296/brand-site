The Customer
Mara.

She arrives on Saturday afternoons. She is in no hurry.

She picks things up, puts them down, picks them up again. She is not deciding whether she can afford it. She is deciding whether it belongs in her life.

She finds busy soundscapes irritating. She needs the room to feel like it has time.

Typical visit 18–24 minutes
Frequency 2× per month, Saturday afternoons
Decision style Deliberate. Needs space to feel certain.
Audio response Responds to sound that does not compete
Peak engagement When the environment feels unhurried
She stays 22% longer when the room feels unhurried.
Musical prescription
TEMPO  68–82 BPM MODE  Major, soft voicings RHYTHM  Acoustic, no drive LYRICAL  Ambient or abstract
