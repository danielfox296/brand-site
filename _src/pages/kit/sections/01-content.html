Kit

Additive primitives for breaking up dense marketing pages. Every block below is a candidate for the comparison pages, vertical pages, and trust pages. Nothing here changes existing layouts — opt in by classname.

Notes

What this kit is

A reference for rebuilding marketing pages without inventing new components every time. Every block above is in styles.css under the KIT v2 section.

What’s deliberately NOT here yet: section-padding clamp, body font-weight bump, and the .card-grid default rewrite. Those touch existing pages and need a separate decision once the kit is approved.
