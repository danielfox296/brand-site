Entuned Files Provisional Patent for First Data-Driven System to Measure and Optimize Retail Music Against Store Performance

Denver startup develops method to correlate musical variables with foot traffic, dwell time, and conversion in physical retail environments

DENVER, CO — April 8, 2026 — Entuned LLC today announced the filing of a provisional patent application covering a systematic method for optimizing music in physical retail environments based on measurable behavioral outcomes. The patent describes a process for deploying parameter-controlled audio, measuring customer response through retail analytics platforms including RetailNext, and continuously refining musical variables to improve store performance.

Forty years of peer-reviewed research in environmental psychology establishes that musical variables—including tempo, familiarity, lyrical content, dynamic compression, and instrumentation—meaningfully influence customer behavior in physical retail settings, including walking pace, time spent in store, and purchase intent. Until now, retailers have lacked the technical capability to systematically test these variables in their own locations and correlate them with measurable outcomes.

Entuned's patented process addresses this gap. By integrating with existing retail analytics infrastructure, the system enables retailers to specify musical parameters, deploy optimized audio, measure behavioral outcomes in real time, and continuously refine the in-store audio environment based on store-specific data.

"Retailers have been influencing customers with music for decades. What's never existed is the ability to measure it and optimize it store by store. We built this because the gap between what the research says music can do and what retailers can actually act on is enormous." — Daniel Fox, Founder & CEO, Entuned

Fox spent three months working hands-on in a high-end retail environment prior to founding Entuned, where he identified the disconnect between existing behavioral research and retailers' practical ability to apply it.

Entuned is currently seeking pilot partnerships with mid-to-large retail chains to validate the platform and build predictive correlation maps across store formats and customer segments.

About Entuned: Entuned is a Denver-based retail optimization platform that generates parameter-controlled music for physical retail environments and correlates musical variables with measurable behavioral outcomes including foot traffic, dwell time, and conversion. The company integrates with retail analytics platforms including RetailNext to provide retailers with data-driven insight into how music affects store performance. Entuned is privately held and currently in pilot development. entuned.co
