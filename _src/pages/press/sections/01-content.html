Standard paragraph

Entuned is a retail music strategy platform that turns customer psychology into store soundtracks — driving measurable sales lift. It composes original music — using generative tools — for multi-location specialty retailers, then measures its effect against the behavioral data stores already collect. Because the music is original, there is no third-party PRO licensing to clear. It answers the question the catalog music industry never could: does a store's music actually change how the store performs? Entuned was founded by Daniel Fox, who bootstrapped Skreened to eight-figure annual revenue before exiting. The company is based in Denver, Colorado. More at entuned.co.

People

Daniel Fox — Founder

Daniel Fox is the founder of Entuned. He bootstrapped Skreened to eight-figure annual revenue in six years — 180 employees, multiple production facilities, and in 2013 dual recognition as one of Ohio's fastest-growing companies and one of its best places to work. After exiting, he returned to music as a technical discipline, working as a producer and composer to build a working model of how specific musical choices shape customer behavior in stores. That model became Entuned.

Mrinmayi Katti — Measurement

Mrinmayi Katti owns the measurement side of every Entuned pilot. She previously built distributed data-migration workflows that moved 200K+ records per run at 99%+ fidelity (Loxo) and A/B testing work that contributed to an 18% retention lift at scale (Reliance Jio). When a retailer asks whether the music moved conversion in a specific store on a specific day, she is the person who can answer.

On the research

Entuned's work is grounded in published, replicated findings on music and shopper behavior — Areni and Kim (1993) on classical music and price selection in a wine store, Milliman (1982, 1986) on tempo, and North and Hargreaves through the 1990s and 2000s. These are cited, public, peer-reviewed studies; we're glad to point you to the primary sources rather than have you take our word for it.
