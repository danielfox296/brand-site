What Mood Media charges

The pricing, from Mood's own page

Mood Media's self-serve plans are listed at Fundamentals $17/mo ($12.75/mo annual), Signature $35/mo ($26.25/mo annual), and Engage $40.15/mo ($30.42/mo annual), per location and per zone. Enterprise pricing is by quote. Self-serve plans use the "Mood Harmony" player (a $139 device, free with annual plans) or BYOD via the Mood Media app. Mood also resells the Pandora for Business channel at $29.95/mo monthly or $23.96/mo annual.

Credit where it's due

What Mood gets right

Mood Media is the largest in-store audio provider in the world and has been doing this for decades. They bundle the PRO licenses (ASCAP, BMI, SESAC, GMR) directly into the subscription so a single-location operator can play licensed catalog music without filing separately. Their catalog spans most of what a mainstream retail buyer would expect to hear, and their hardware and field-service operation can support enterprise rollouts at a scale most competitors can't match. If you want a known brand and a fully bundled licensing posture, that case has merit.

For owner-operators

Where Mood falls short

  • Auto-renewing subscriptions, end-of-period cancellation. Per Mood's own terms, subscriptions auto-renew, cancellation takes effect only at the end of the current paid period, and pre-paid services are non-refundable. (Mood Media terms)
  • Per-location AND per-zone billing. A location with two zones (front of house + back patio, retail floor + fitting rooms) pays for two subscriptions, with no published volume discount across zones inside a single store.
  • BBB shows individual billing complaints. Mood's BBB profile documents specific cancellation/billing failures — including a $1,261.57 erroneous bill in November 2022 after a September 2021 cancellation confirmation, and a charge for an extra month after a February 2024 cancel request. These are individual complaints, not a class action, but the pattern of "cancel didn't actually stop the billing" comes up. (BBB complaints)
  • No published in-store sales-lift methodology. Mood markets atmosphere benefits but we could not find a published methodology for measuring the basket-size or dwell-time lift their music produces in your store specifically.
  • You don't own the music. Catalog programming means the same songs play in competitors' stores. If your customer just heard "Brand X's playlist" at the mall before walking into yours, the audio is doing identity work for someone else.

Common questions

Switching from Mood Media

Can I keep my Mood Media account while testing Entuned?
Yes. Entuned Free is $0 with no card, so you can run it in one zone or one store as a parallel test while your Mood subscription is still active. We recommend doing exactly that for a couple of weeks before you start the cancellation paperwork on Mood — it makes the comparison concrete and gives you something to point at internally.
Will I owe ASCAP, BMI, SESAC, or GMR if I switch to Entuned?
No, not for music played through Entuned. We compose and own every track in our catalog outright, so there is no PRO obligation passed to the operator for the audio Entuned delivers. If you also play other music in the location (live performers, a personal Spotify account on a back office speaker, etc.), those sources keep their own licensing posture — Entuned only covers what plays through Entuned.
How fast can I roll Entuned out across all my locations?
Each location takes minutes. The player is a URL on a browser-capable device at music.entuned.co. There is no hardware to ship, no install appointment, no field tech. Multi-location operators typically self-serve through app.entuned.co/start and use the customer dashboard to manage all sites from one login.
How do I actually cancel Mood Media without getting billed for an extra period?
Per Mood's terms, cancellation only takes effect at the end of the current paid period, and pre-paid services are non-refundable. Send the cancellation in writing, save the confirmation, watch your card statement for the next two cycles. We've written a more detailed playbook here: Getting out of a Mood Media contract.
Is there a usage limit on Entuned Free?
Entuned Free is a real free tier — no card, no trial timer, intended for ongoing use by single-location operators who don't need the deeper customization or measurement of the paid tiers. The differences live in the pricing page; nothing on the free tier expires.
What if my customers expect the songs they hear in mall stores?
That's the right question to interrogate. The honest answer is most of those customers don't consciously notice what's playing — they notice whether it fits the store. Entuned tunes to your customer's psychology rather than chart familiarity, and operators who switch usually report customers describing the store as "feeling more put together," not "missing the songs from the mall."
