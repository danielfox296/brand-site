How Music Affects Customer Behavior in Retail Stores

Your store probably plays music. Most do. But there's a wide gap between "having music on" and using sound as a deliberate tool to influence how people shop.

Key Takeaways

• Slow tempo + minor key increases dwell time and spending — not slow tempo alone
• Genre match increases purchase intent. Classic study: French music → customers chose French wine over German 5-to-1
• Lyrical content primes purchasing decisions at a subconscious level
• Most stores have no way to measure what their music is doing — that’s the gap
