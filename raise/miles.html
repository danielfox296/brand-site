Miles

Adaptive intelligence that plays the room.

Written for self-expression Unknown tempo Semantic noise No feedback loop

Licensed music was never designed for this.

Every track on a retail playlist was written by an artist solving a different problem. Self-expression. Commercial appeal. Artistic vision. None of those goals align with what a store actually needs.

The result is noise. Not acoustic noise. Data noise. Every variable that matters to behavioral outcomes is uncontrolled. Tempo is a guess. Lyrics carry unintended meaning. Sonic texture clashes with brand. And there's no signal traveling back.

The playlist plays. Customers move through the space. Sales happen or they don't. Nobody knows why.

A closed loop between music and commerce.

Miles sees what's happening in the store. Foot traffic. Dwell time. Transactions. Time of day. Weather outside. It processes that data against everything it has learned from every store in the network.

Then it acts.

Miles generates the next track in near real-time. Parameters tuned to the current state of the room. Transition seamless. No interruption. No human in the loop unless they want one.

The store doesn't see Miles working. They see sales going up.

Two kinds of intelligence. One system.

Fleet Learning
Store Learning
Cultural and semantic parameters that work here.

Some things work everywhere. The relationship between tempo and attention. Volume curves that extend dwell without fatigue. These are physiological. Miles learns them across the entire fleet.

Other things are local. The neighborhood. The brand. The customer who walks in at 4pm on a Wednesday versus 11am on a Saturday. These are cultural, semantic, specific. Miles learns them store by store.

Both loops run continuously. Both get sharper over time. The fleet makes every store smarter. Every store makes the fleet smarter.

Every parameter. Intentional.

Intentional. Measurable. Learnable.

Generative music means every detail is a choice. Tempo isn't inherited from whatever the artist felt like. Lyrics aren't semantic accidents. Harmonic rhythm, rhythmic density, timbral texture, energy arc. All specified, all measurable, all learnable.

This is what makes the loop trainable. Without control over the input variables, there's nothing to correlate. Nothing to optimize. Nothing to learn from.

Miles can only exist because the music was built to be understood.
