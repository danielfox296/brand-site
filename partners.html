THE PROBLEM

The audio is always the afterthought.

Lighting gets a plan. Fixtures get a budget. Product placement follows a merchandising strategy. Visual elements are deliberate down to the paint color.

The music is a Spotify playlist someone made three months ago.

Research going back to Milliman (1982) has consistently shown that in-store music changes customer behavior. Tempo affects how long people stay. Genre congruence with brand identity affects what they spend. Environmental fit shifts how customers perceive the store itself.

Almost nobody in independent retail treats audio as a controllable variable. Your clients are leaving a behavioral lever on the floor.

THE PLATFORM

Original music, composed around customer psychology.

Entuned profiles a store’s ideal customer psychographically, then composes original music built around that profile. Tempo, mode, instrumentation, genre congruence, lyrical density. Every parameter maps to a specific behavioral outcome.

No playlist. No catalog. No licensed tracks carrying someone else’s brand associations.

Original music, built from customer psychology, with no licensing liability. Plays in-browser. No hardware. No app install. $99/month per location.

1 Profile the store’s ideal customer (psychographic, not demographic)
2 Translate that psychology into musical parameters (tempo, mode, instrumentation, genre congruence)
3 Compose original music to those parameters
4 Stream continuously in-store via web player

Frequently Asked Questions

How does commission work?

You earn 20% of the monthly subscription for every client who signs up through your referral link and converts to paid after the free period. Commission is recurring for as long as the client stays subscribed.

When do I get paid?

Monthly, after the client’s payment processes. Minimum payout threshold: $50. Paid via Stripe Connect or PayPal.

What if my client cancels?

Commission stops when the subscription stops. If they resubscribe, commission resumes.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You make the introduction. Entuned handles everything from onboarding to support. The player runs in-browser with no hardware required.

What does Boost include that Free doesn’t?

Boost includes psychographic profiling of the store’s ideal customer and music composed to match that profile. The Free tier uses broad behavioral modes without ICP customization.

Can I white-label Entuned?

Not currently. We’re open to discussing that with high-volume partners.
