What you get when your wave opens.

Essentials is the free tier. You pick one outcome — Increase Dwell or Infuse Energy — and the music starts streaming through whatever speakers you already have. No card required, no time limit, indefinite access.

  • 100+ song library
  • Two outcome modes — Increase Dwell, Infuse Energy
  • One mode active at a time, manually selected
  • General catalogue, not customized for your customer
  • PRO-indemnified from day one
Want music tailored to your specific customer? Core ($99/mo) builds the catalogue around your Ideal Customer Profile. Professional ($399/mo) adds POS-driven self-improving refinement. Both unlock when your wave opens.
