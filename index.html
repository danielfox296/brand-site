The Gap

The music in your store is doing something. Almost nobody chose what.

Most stores are either paying a vendor on autopilot, running someone's personal Spotify into the speakers, or playing whatever the radio left on. The team that picked the lighting had a plan. The team that picked the paint had a plan. The audio is whatever ended up there.

Forty years of peer-reviewed research says that's a measurable miss. Tempo affects the pace customers walk through your store (Milliman 1982). Music a customer reads as belonging to them moves their willingness to pay (Areni & Kim 1993). Familiar songs cut perceived dwell (Yalch & Spangenberg 2000). The variable is doing work in the room. The question is whether you chose what.

The Lever

Music engineered for your customer and your outcome.

The behavioral variable is parameterizable. Tempo, key, instrumentation, lyrical density, energy arc, and cultural reference set are all things that can be specified on purpose to fit a specific customer profile and a specific desired outcome — dwell, basket, conversion, brand reinforcement.

Entuned starts from the customer your store is built for and produces music against those parameters. Not picked from a catalogue. Not curated from a playlist library. Engineered. The tracks are original, commercial-use cleared, and PRO-indemnified the moment they play.

The Compound

Self-improving as the data accumulates.

The Professional tier reads sales and traffic data tied to specific environmental conditions in your stores and refines the music against your store's outcomes as the dataset grows. Self-improving refinement compounds over weeks of data, not days.

Every store running self-improving refinement adds to the proprietary behavioral dataset that improves the system for that store. The Entuned Free and Core tiers prove the concept on the floor. Professional proves the lift in the report.

Common questions, before you start.

How much does it cost?
Entuned Free — no card, no time limit. Core is $99 per location per month. Professional is $399 per location per month. Enterprise is negotiated per-store for multi-fleet rollouts. See full pricing →
Do I need new hardware?
No. Entuned streams through whatever audio system you already have — Sonos, AirPlay, Bluetooth, line-in, hardwired. The stream plays from a URL we hand you.
What about music licensing — am I covered?
Yes. Every track Entuned plays is original, commercial-use cleared, and PRO-indemnified the moment it starts. No ASCAP, BMI, SESAC, or SoundExchange royalties. Every subscription includes a written indemnification certificate. Full details on the licensing page →
