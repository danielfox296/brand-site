The short answer

Yes. The research is older and more specific than most shop owners realize.

Music isn't ambient — it's a behavioral variable, peer-reviewed since 1982. Tempo controls how customers move through your store. Identity-fit music lifts what they're willing to pay. Familiar songs drive them out faster, even when they say they like it. The receipts are below.

The mechanism

Background music has been a creative endeavor for decades. We turned it into a design spec.

Background music vendors lease songs artists wrote for other reasons — and the lyrics, tempo, energy, and cultural reference were never chosen with your retail floor in mind. Entuned produces original music to specification: your customer, your category, your outcome. Tempo tuned to your floor pace. Identity calibrated to your customer. Lyrics screened to avoid actively negative-priming the people you're trying to convert.

Linger longer

Tempo, key, and energy shaped to slow customer pace and extend dwell.

Larger basket size

Identity-fit programming that lifts what customers are willing to spend.

Higher conversion

Energy arc and lyrical density tuned to the decision moment on your floor.

Brand reinforcement

Original music your store actually owns — never plays in a competitor's store.

The proof

Music that gets sharper the longer it runs.

Free and Boost deliver the concept. Pro pulls sales and traffic data from your locations and refines the music against the outcome you set — the longer it runs, the more precisely it produces what you measure. Self-improving refinement compounds over weeks and months of correlation.
