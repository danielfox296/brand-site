Founding Retailer Terms

Last updated: April 16, 2026

These terms apply when you subscribe to the Founding Retailer plan on entuned.co. They form a binding agreement between you and Entuned LLC ("Entuned," "we," "us"), a Colorado limited liability company. If you do not agree to these terms, do not subscribe.

1. The service

Entuned delivers an original audio stream built for your store. The stream plays over your existing in-store audio system via a streaming URL we provide. We refresh the composition monthly. You control playback.

2. Subscription and billing

Pricing, billing, and related terms:

  • Price. $99 per location per month, billed monthly in advance through Stripe.
  • Automatic renewal. Your subscription renews every month until cancelled.
  • Per-location. One subscription covers one physical retail location. Multiple locations require multiple subscriptions; you can set quantity at checkout.
  • Taxes. Sales and use taxes are added where applicable and collected at checkout.
  • Payment failures. If a payment fails we will retry and notify you. Access to the stream may be suspended if a payment remains unresolved for 14 days.

3. Founding Retailer benefits

Three things set the Founding plan apart from our standard offering:

  • Price lock. The $99 per location per month rate is locked for 12 months from your subscription start date.
  • After 12 months. Your subscription continues at our then-standard rate unless we agree otherwise in writing.
  • Cap. The Founding Retailer program is limited to the first 20 locations. Once filled, new subscriptions move to standard pricing.

4. Cancellation and refunds

You may cancel at any time from your customer portal or by emailing hello@entuned.co. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you retain access through that date. We do not pro-rate or refund unused portions of a billing period. If you believe you were charged in error, email hello@entuned.co within 14 days and we will review.

5. License to the music

During an active subscription, Entuned grants you a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to play the audio we deliver in the physical retail location or locations covered by your subscription. You may play it for customers, staff, and anyone present in the store. You may not re-stream or rebroadcast the audio outside your store, record or distribute it, resell it, or use it in advertising, video content, or other media without our written permission. When your subscription ends, the license ends and you must stop playing the audio.

6. Ownership

Entuned retains all right, title, and interest in the audio, the underlying compositions, the production process, and any analytics or methods Entuned uses internally. You retain all right, title, and interest in your brand, store data, and customer data. Feedback you provide, including suggestions, requests, and notes during intake, may be used by Entuned to improve the service without further obligation to you.

7. Music licensing and PRO fees

All audio Entuned delivers is generated by Entuned and is cleared for commercial in-store use. You do not owe separate performing rights organization fees (ASCAP, BMI, SESAC, GMR) for the music Entuned provides. If a rights organization questions this, contact us and we will provide supporting documentation.

8. Data

If you share transaction, traffic, or sensor data with us during the Founding program, we use it in aggregated, anonymized form to improve the audio and the product. We collect basic stream usage data such as start time, duration, and location to operate the service. We do not sell your data. Our general data practices are described in our privacy policy.
