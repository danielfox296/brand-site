Subscription Terms

Last updated: May 9, 2026

These terms apply when you subscribe to or activate any tier of Entuned (Entuned Free, Core, Professional, or Enterprise) on entuned.co. They form a binding agreement between you and Entuned LLC ("Entuned," "we," "us"), a Wyoming limited liability company. If you do not agree to these terms, do not subscribe or activate the service.

1. The service

Entuned delivers an original audio stream engineered for your store. The stream plays over your existing in-store audio system via a streaming URL we provide. The level of customization, the size and refresh of the catalogue, the available outcomes, and the integration depth depend on the tier you are subscribed to. You control playback.

2. Tiers and pricing

Entuned offers four service tiers with the following commercial terms:

  • Entuned Free. Free, indefinite access. General catalogue, two outcome modes (Linger, Lift Energy), one mode active at a time. No customer-profile customization. Subject to fair use limits for the audio stream.
  • Core. $99 per location per month, billed monthly in advance through Stripe. Includes ICP-tailored music for one Ideal Customer Profile, all research-backed outcomes, regular catalogue refresh and culling.
  • Professional. $399 per location per month, billed monthly in advance through Stripe. Includes everything in Core plus POS integration, Outcome Scheduling, interstitial messaging, multiple ICPs, and self-improving refinement.
  • Enterprise. Negotiated per-location pricing under a separate written agreement. Custom ICP design, performance terms, and rollout details are specified in that agreement and supersede these public terms where they differ.
  • Per-location. One paid subscription covers one physical retail location. Multiple locations require multiple subscriptions; quantity is set at checkout.
  • Taxes. Sales and use taxes are added where applicable and collected at checkout.
  • Automatic renewal. Paid subscriptions renew every month until cancelled. Entuned Free does not renew because no charge occurs.
  • Payment failures. If a payment fails we will retry and notify you. Access to the paid stream may be suspended if a payment remains unresolved for 14 days. The Entuned Free catalogue remains available.

3. Tier changes

You may upgrade or downgrade your tier at any time from your customer portal or by emailing hello@entuned.co. Upgrades take effect immediately and are pro-rated against the current billing period. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period; no pro-rated refund is issued. Downgrading may reduce the level of customization, integration, or service available — for example, downgrading from Professional to Core ends POS integration, Outcome Scheduling, and self-improving refinement against your store's data.

4. Cancellation and refunds

You may cancel any paid subscription at any time from your customer portal or by emailing hello@entuned.co. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you retain access through that date. We do not pro-rate or refund unused portions of a billing period. If you believe you were charged in error, email hello@entuned.co within 14 days and we will review. Entuned Free access may be discontinued at our discretion if the account is inactive for an extended period or if fair use limits are exceeded.

During an active subscription (including the free Entuned Free tier), Entuned grants you a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to play the audio we deliver in the physical retail location or locations covered by your subscription. You may play it for customers, staff, and anyone present in the store. You may not re-stream or rebroadcast the audio outside your store, record or distribute it, resell it, or use it in advertising, video content, or other media without our written permission. When your subscription ends or your account is terminated, the license ends and you must stop playing the audio.

Entuned retains all right, title, and interest in the audio, the underlying compositions, the production process, and any analytics or methods Entuned uses internally. You retain all right, title, and interest in your brand, store data, and customer data. Feedback you provide — including suggestions, requests, and notes during intake — may be used by Entuned to improve the service without further obligation to you.

7. PRO indemnification and music licensing

All audio Entuned delivers is generated by Entuned and is cleared for commercial in-store use. You do not owe separate performing rights organization fees (ASCAP, BMI, SESAC, GMR) for the music Entuned provides. Every Entuned subscription, including Entuned Free, includes a written indemnification certificate that identifies the music source as original Entuned compositions and lists Entuned as the indemnifying party. If a performing rights organization or other third party asserts that music played through Entuned in your store requires a license, Entuned defends the claim and covers any associated costs. Multi-location operators on the Professional or Enterprise tier receive a bulk certificate covering the full fleet. Indemnification applies to music played through the Entuned system during your operating hours and does not extend to other audio sources you choose to play alongside or instead of Entuned.

8. Data

We use aggregated, anonymized signal across customers to improve the underlying engine. We do not sell your data. We collect basic stream usage data such as start time, duration, and location to operate the service across all tiers. Our general data practices are described in our privacy policy.
