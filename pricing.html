Questions

Why is Entuned Free actually free?
The Entuned Free catalogue is real and yours forever — no trial clock, no credit card, no expiration. The structural difference at Core is not artificial scarcity — it is that Core music is built around your specific customer rather than a general catalogue. Most operators move to Core when their floor is ready for that level of specificity.
Do I need special hardware?
No. Entuned streams through whatever audio system you already have — Sonos, AirPlay, Bluetooth, line-in, or a hardwired speaker rig.
What about music licensing and PRO fees?
All audio Entuned delivers is original and commercial-use cleared. No PRO fees, no catalogue licensing, no public-performance exposure. Every subscription includes a written indemnification certificate. Full details on the licensing page.
Can I cancel?
Yes, monthly. No long-term contract on Core or Professional. Enterprise pricing is negotiated as part of the agreement.
What's the difference between Core and Professional?
Our Core offering builds music around one Ideal Customer Profile and gives you all manual outcome control. Professional adds POS integration, multiple ICPs (Professional tailors music to each distinct customer type your store serves, instead of a single profile), Outcome Scheduling, scheduled interstitial promotional messaging, and self-improving refinement that compounds using your store's POS data. Most single-store shop owners start on Core. Multi-location retailers with a real measurement stack start on Professional.
I already pay Soundtrack Your Brand or Mood Media. How does this compare?
Those vendors license catalogue music and pass the cost through your subscription. The catalogue was written for an artist's self-expression, not for your store's customer. Entuned generates original music engineered to specific parameters — tempo, key, lyrics, all for the customer your store is built to serve. Entuned is a different product, a sales lever.
