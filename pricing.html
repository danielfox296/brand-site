Questions

Why is Founding pricing so much lower than standard?
The first 20 locations help us refine the product across real retail environments. In exchange for deploying early, you lock in $99 per location per month for 12 months. After 20 locations, standard pricing takes effect.
Do I need special hardware?
No. Entuned streams through your existing in-store audio system.
What about music licensing?
All music is generated by Entuned. You are fully cleared for commercial in-store use. No PRO fees, no catalog licensing, no legal exposure.
Can I cancel?
Yes, monthly. No long-term contract on the Founding plan.
I have 5 or more locations. Should I do Founding or Pilot?
Pilot. It is structured around measuring lift across locations, which is where the real value of Entuned shows up. Founding is for retailers who want in now without the pilot overhead.
