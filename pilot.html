Run a measured pilot in your stores.

A pilot is a structured measurement period. Usually 90 days. Sometimes six months. You get original music in your stores, we get a clean read on what moved.

The Process

How a pilot runs

Week 1

Discovery Call

Sixty minutes with you and your team. We learn your stores, your customer, your current audio setup, and what you are trying to move on the P&L. No tech demo.

Weeks 2–3

Music Build and Review

We compose original music for your stores. You listen, you give notes, we adjust. Nothing goes live until you have signed off on what your customers will hear.

Week 4

Go Live

An iPad connects to your existing speakers. You press play. That is the install.

Weeks 4–12

Measurement Window

The new music runs in your test stores. Your other stores stay on whatever you are playing today. We watch the numbers for the agreed window.

Week 13

Results and Next Steps

We sit down together and walk through what moved and what did not. If the numbers justify it, we talk about a rollout. If they do not, you keep the data and we shake hands.

What we watch

What gets measured

Your test stores against matched control stores. If you run a single location, we compare the measurement window against your own trailing baseline.

Average Dwell Time

How long customers stay in the store.

Average Transaction Value

Average ticket and units per transaction.

Items Per Transaction

Conversion rate against traffic.

Foot Traffic Patterns

Traffic patterns by hour and by day. Dwell and conversion broken out against your peak periods.

Return Visit Frequency

Return visits inside the window, pulled from loyalty data where you have it.

What you bring

What you need to bring

iPad or Tablet with WiFi

A tablet per location, or we ship one. The app we provide runs on any recent iPad.

Bluetooth or AirPlay Speakers

Your existing speakers. Most commercial setups work without modification. If yours is unusual, we will tell you on the discovery call.

Two Comparable Store Locations

At least one test store and one control store with similar traffic, demographics, and mix. Single-location operators run a before-and-after instead.

Access to POS & Analytics Data

Access to your point-of-sale exports and traffic counts for the measurement window. If you already have RetailNext or a similar platform, that is what we pull from.

60–90 Minutes for ICP Workshop

One 60-minute working session with someone who knows your customer and your stores. That is the only meeting we ask for upfront.

FAQ

Common Questions

What if I only have one location?

We can. Single-location operators run a before-and-after window. We measure your trailing baseline, then measure the pilot window, and compare them directly.

What speakers work?

Most Bluetooth and AirPlay setups work. Most wired commercial speakers work. If yours is proprietary, tell us on the discovery call and we will work out the connection.

Who manages the music day-to-day?

The app runs on its own once you press play. Your staff does nothing. We handle music changes from our side.

Can staff override the music?

Yes. There are pause and skip controls in the app if a staff member needs them. In practice nobody touches it.

What if the pilot doesn't show results?

You keep the data and the music that ran during the pilot stays with you through the end of the window. No ongoing obligation.

How much data do I get?

A weekly email with what we are seeing so far. A written readout at the end of the measurement window covering dwell, conversion, basket, traffic, and return visits against your control.
